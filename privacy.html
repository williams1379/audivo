Audivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Audivo is developed by William Sutanto. For privacy questions, contact audivoapp.support@gmail.com.

Microphone and audio processing

Audivo accesses your microphone or a supported external audio input with your permission. While the recorder screen is open, input monitoring may process audio to display levels and support Sound Check without saving a recording. When you start a recording session, Audivo saves the received audio on your device. A session you start can continue through a microphone foreground service while you use another app or turn off the screen, with an ongoing notification.

Audio adjustments such as input trim, compression, and noise reduction are processed on your device. Audivo does not automatically upload your audio to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Audivo stores recordings, session names, timestamps, recording settings, markers, session events, summaries, and diagnostic information locally. Diagnostic information can include app and Android versions, input device names and routing, audio configuration, storage status, and errors. Settings and selected folder information are also stored locally.

Completed recordings may be copied to the default destination or a folder you select. Internal audio and recovery files may remain in app storage and use additional space. Export folders are organized by the recording start date. File preparation follows the selected recording format; playback uses the original audio files.

Sharing and destination folders

Audivo shares audio files or diagnostic reports with another app only when you choose a sharing action. The receiving app handles that information under its own practices. A storage provider you choose may synchronize exported files to a cloud service according to that provider's settings. Audivo does not control those services or copies you have already shared.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

The current Audivo app does not require an account and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not automatically send recordings or diagnostic reports to the developer and does not sell your recordings.

Permissions

Microphone permission enables input monitoring and recording. Notification and microphone foreground service permissions support visible ongoing recording. A wake lock helps the device continue processing an active session. On older Android versions, storage permission may be requested to save recordings in shared storage; on supported versions, a system folder picker grants access to the destination you select. You can manage permissions in Android settings; restricting them can disable related features.

Retention and deletion

Local recordings and related information remain until you delete them or remove the app's local data. The Library provides recording deletion; where offered, you can also request deletion of exported copies. Exported files, provider backups, and files already shared to other apps may remain and should be managed separately. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can permanently remove recordings that have not been exported.

Support correspondence

If you contact support, William Sutanto receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate reported issues, and is retained only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Email providers process correspondence under their own terms. Do not send sensitive recordings or identity documents unless necessary. Contact the support address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Security and recording responsibility

Local app storage is subject to Android's device and application protections. Audivo does not provide a separate encrypted recording vault. Protect access to your device and any exported files. Obtain any consent required before recording other people.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if Audivo's data practices change. For questions or requests, email audivoapp.support@gmail.com.
